Vietnamese lunar dates for Google Calendar, calculated on Vietnam time (UTC+7). 1 Jan 2026 to 31 Dec 2065.

Add to Google Calendar Add to Apple Calendar

Add it by hand (Google Calendar on a computer)

  1. Open calendar.google.com.
  2. On the left, next to Other calendars, click + then From URL.
  3. Paste the link above and click Add calendar.
  4. Google can take a few hours to show it the first time.

What the labels mean

15/8Lunar day 15, month 8
1/9 ÂLMùng 1 of month 9
15/7 RằmRằm (full moon day)
1/4NDay 1 of the leap 4th month (N = nhuận)
1/1 Tết Đinh MùiTết, with the year's name
29/12 Giao thừaLast day of the lunar year

Holidays: Tết (mùng 1 to 3), Rằm tháng Giêng, Tết Hàn Thực, Giỗ Tổ Hùng Vương, Lễ Phật Đản, Tết Đoan Ngọ, Vu Lan, Trung Thu, Ông Công Ông Táo, Giao thừa.
